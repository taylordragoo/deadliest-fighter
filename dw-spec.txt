.................................< OVERVIEW >............................(2.0)

Deadliest Warrior is based on a popular Spike tv series in which two very 
different warriors are put to the test to discover which one would win in a 
one on one fight. The game is a variation of a fighting game in which you 
play the role of a warrior from the show and battle other warriors in a 
semi-gladiator style battle.

Players can play singlplayer modes, such as arcade mode and battle mode, or
multiplayer, local or online. There are three difficulty modes to choose from 
(normal, hard, and deadliest). The game has trophy support and a good deal of 
unlockable equipment, which can keep you busy for a while. Also, there is to 
be several packs of downloadable content starting early 2011. 
 
.................................< GAMEPLAY >............................(3.0)

Gameplay is not your standard fighting game fare. For starters, the damage 
is very high so you have to be carful not to get hit. Also, many things can 
cause you to straight die, such as having your sword arm, head, or legs cut 
off (or getting hit in the head with any manner of ranged weapon). Timing is 
everything and it is important to learn some defensive skills, like parrying   
and dodging, if you plan on doing well. It is most important that you know 
your warrior and play to his strengths. 

-------------------< CLASSES >--------------------------------------(3.1)

There are three classes of warrior listed in the game. These are only a basic 
disciption of how a warrior will fight. There are still many other variables 
to consider when playing. I'll go over these in more depth in the warriors 
section.

CHAMPIONS: Balanced fighters with high armor and decent 
damage, but have slower movement speed.
(Spartan, Centurian, Knight, Samurai)

BERZERKERS: Heavy damage and moderate speed. Higher 
capacity for dodging, but lower armor.
(Pirate, Viking)

GEURRILLAS: Low damage but high speed, capable of
tumbling, practically no armor. 
(Apache, Ninja) 

----------------------------< OFFENSE >-----------------------------(3.2.0)

In order to win in any fighting game your gonna have to go on the offense.
In Deadliest Warrior there are many ways to go about this. You can stab.
You can slash. You can even beat your enemies heads in. Here is all you 
need to know about the offense systems.

=======================< ATTACK RANGES >======================(3.2.1)

SHORT RANGE: Uses your short range weapon, fastest of the
attacks but as the title states range is short.
(swords, knives, etc...)

MEDIUM RANGE: Uses your mid range weapon, slower but keeps
your opponent at bay, higher damage.
(polearms, spears, etc...) 

LONG RANGE: Uses your long range weapon, can do substantial damage but 
is relativly easy to dodge, some ranged weapons have poison which
damages over time. With thrown weapons if the attack misses or is blocked
you can recover the weapon with the L3 button. Also, if your opponent misses 
or you block his attack you can break his weapon so he cannot pick it up.
(bows, javalins, etc...)

If your opponent has a shield he'll be able to block all incoming ranged
weapons with minimum damage. You may want to try and break his shield arm 
to open up his defense. Otherwise, wait for the opertune moment to strike. 

ALTERNATE: Each warrior has a special weapon they can use. This
attack is unblockable and can trigger an instant kill if used at the right 
moment. 
(special weapon depends on the warrior)

=======================< ATTACK ZONES >=======================(3.2.2)

HIGH: targets the head doing a good deal of damage, has
the chance to decapitate, 

MIDDLE: Targets the torso doing moderate damage, chance to 
damage or amputate arms causing the opponent to lose the use
of certain abilities, such as shields and ranged attacks. 

LOW: Targets the legs doing lower damage, chance to damage
or amputate legs, damaged legs slow the opponent down and 
take away his ability to dodge, amputated legs are an instant
kill as your opponent can't walk.

=======================< ATTACK TYPES >=======================(3.2.3)

BASHING: Good against armored opponents and those with shields,
usually has some manner of knockback, makes a great squishy bone
crunching smack when it hits opponents. 
(cannot cut off limbs)

SLASHING: Not very effective against armored opponents, but is 
great for carving them up. (chop-em up)

PIERCING: Effective against most armor types, can cause a lot of
damage especially when connecting with the face or chest. 
(cannot cut off limbs)

EXPLOSIVE: Exclusive to the pirate, effective against everyting,
(send limbs flying)

Each warrior also has a special finish that they can perform by 
using their special attack when the opponents lifebar is critcal.
This is signaled by a heart beating sound and the lifebar glowing red.
These can be pretty entertaining, not to mention frustrating for your 
friends.
